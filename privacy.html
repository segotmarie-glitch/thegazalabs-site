Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

This policy describes what information Gaza OS (the "API Client") accesses, collects, stores and uses, how and why, and what you can do about it. Gaza OS is operated by Marie Segot, 60 rue François 1er, 75008 Paris, France ("we", "us"). Contact: contact@thegazalabs.com.

1. YouTube API Services

Gaza OS uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel to Gaza OS you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and your information is also handled by Google as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Gaza OS's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. Information we access

Only after you authorise Gaza OS on Google's OAuth 2.0 consent screen, and only for the two scopes below:

  • youtube.readonly — we call channels.list once, when you connect, to read the identifier and title of the channel you authorised.
  • youtube.upload — we call videos.insert to upload a video to that channel. This happens only when you click "upload" for a specific video, after you chose its title, description and visibility and confirmed the upload.

Gaza OS does not read your videos, comments, subscribers, analytics or any other YouTube data, and it requests no other scope.

3. Information we store

  • Channel record: the channel identifier, the connection status and the permissions derived from it, stored in our database until you revoke access or ask us to delete them. The channel title (retrieved from YouTube with your authorisation) is deleted automatically 30 days after it was last retrieved; each time you re-authorise Gaza OS the title is retrieved again and the 30 days restart. An account whose title has expired must be re-authorised before it can be used again.
  • OAuth tokens: access and refresh tokens are held in the application's memory only. They are never written to our database or to our logs, and they disappear when the application restarts or when you disconnect the channel.
  • Video files you add: files you choose to add to Gaza OS for upload are kept in Gaza OS's private storage until you or we delete them.
  • Upload result: the identifier and address of the video YouTube created, so you can find it; kept until you ask us to delete it.
  • Account data of Gaza OS operators: an e-mail address, display name, role and a password hash (Argon2id) for people authorised to use Gaza OS. Gaza OS has no public sign-up.

4. How we use it

Solely to provide the features you invoke: showing which channel a video will be uploaded to, and uploading the video you selected with the settings you chose. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your information and we do not share information obtained through YouTube API Services with third parties, except with Google/YouTube as part of the requests you initiate. We do not serve advertisements and Gaza OS contains no third-party analytics or tracking scripts.

6. Revoking access and deleting your data

You can revoke Gaza OS's access to your Google account at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

If you revoke access, or ask us to delete your data by writing to contact@thegazalabs.com, we delete the channel record and any tokens we hold, and the video files you added if you ask, within 7 calendar days.

7. Security

Access to Gaza OS requires an individual account; sessions use HTTP-only cookies and CSRF protection; passwords are stored only as Argon2id hashes; OAuth tokens are never persisted.

8. Children

Gaza OS is not directed to children. When a video is uploaded, the operator is asked to declare whether it is made for kids and is reminded that such content must be declared on YouTube.

9. Changes

If we change how we access, use or share information obtained through YouTube API Services, we will update this page and, where required, ask you to accept the new policy before continuing.
